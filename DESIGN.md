# Design

Light, warm, one accent. Structure over decoration: a tool grid on the home screen, cards on an off-white ground, a four-tab bottom bar.

## Tokens
- Ground `#F6F5F2`, surface `#FFFFFF`, line `#E3E1DC`, divider `#F0EEE9`
- Ink `#16181D`, muted `#5B6069`
- Accent `#0F766E` (teal), accent dark `#0B5A54`, accent tint `#E5F4F1`
- Member colours: `#0F766E`, `#B45309`, `#1D4ED8`, `#6D28D9` (differ in lightness, not only hue)
- Type: Space Grotesk 700 for titles (22 px), Inter for everything else (15 body, 13 secondary, 12 caption, 11 nav)
- Radius 14–16 px on cards and inputs, 22 px on round buttons; 10 px gaps in grids
- Touch targets 44 px minimum; contrast 4.5:1 on all text

## Screens (from the canvas mock-up)
1. **Home / Tools** — greeting, profile avatar, paste-or-pick box, recent tool chips, 3×3 tool grid, family strip, bottom nav.
2. **Family** — live map with member avatars and an "I've reached" button, shared shopping list with who-ticked, this week's shared events, sync status line.
3. **Tool screen (Image to exact KB as the pattern)** — back, before/after, preset row, custom input, option toggles, privacy line, Save and Share buttons pinned at the bottom.
4. **Records** — quick-add box, due-soon list, four tiles (Money, Health, Documents, Home), recent entries.

## Rules
- Every tool screen ends in a result with Share, Save and Undo.
- No sign-in screens, no status bars in mock-ups, no gradient washes, no emoji as icons; stroke icons only.
- Copy in plain words; Indian conventions (₹, lakh, DD/MM, A4) by default.
