# QA walkthrough (airplane mode)

Run on a mid-range Samsung and an iPhone with airplane mode on.

1. Scan a 3-page document, OCR it, compress to 200 KB, share to WhatsApp as a document.
2. Paste a photo from the gallery; make it 50 KB at 35×45 mm; confirm the result says it fits.
3. Merge two PDFs, sign the result, protect it with a password, open it.
4. Add "paid 350 milk" in Records; see it in the ledger and the month total.
5. On a second family phone on the same Wi-Fi, tick an item on the shopping list; it shows on the first phone within 3 s.
6. Turn off Wi-Fi on one phone, add a calendar event on each; turn Wi-Fi on; both events appear on both phones with no duplicates.
7. Set a medicine reminder for 2 minutes ahead; it fires with the phone locked.
8. Open the vault with the decoy PIN; it is empty. Open with the real PIN; documents are there.
9. Kill the app during a video compression; the job finishes and notifies.
10. Switch the UI to Hindi; every screen is translated.
